Straight Bussing support

Free, unofficial campus shuttle tracker for iPhone and the web.

Straight Bussing shows the campus shuttles in Hyde Park, Chicago, on a live map: where each bus is, when the next one reaches your stop, and how to get somewhere by walking and shuttle. It is an unofficial student project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the University or by Passio. Arrival times are predictions and can be wrong.

For safety rides or anything urgent, use the official service.

Report a bug or ask a question

Open an issue at github.com/blobberus/straight-bussing/issues (a free GitHub account is needed). It helps to include:

  1. iPhone app or web app, and the version (bottom of About in the app);
  2. your iPhone model and iOS version, or your browser;
  3. the screen, the route or stop, and the time it happened;
  4. what you expected and what you saw (a screenshot helps; crop out anything private).

Common questions

  • No buses on the map. The shuttles may not be running right now; check the route’s hours in Routes. If the live feed is late or down, a banner under the search bar says so and times are marked as approximate.
  • Are the times exact? No. Arrival times come from the live shuttle feed; ride, wait and total times are estimates from schedules and live predictions, marked “est.” with their source.
  • Simulated buses (demo). In the iPhone app, Settings has a switch that shows made-up buses, so you can try every feature when no shuttle runs. While it is on, every screen says “Demo mode: simulated buses”; it turns off when the app restarts. Turn it off to see live buses again.
  • Location. Optional. The app asks only when you tap “Use my location”; without it you can search a station or a place.
  • Bus alerts and the Lock Screen. Bus alerts and the trip Live Activity update while the app is open; alerts on a locked phone would need a push server, which is planned.
