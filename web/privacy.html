Privacy policy

Straight Bussing, for iPhone and the web. Effective October 10, 2026.

Straight Bussing is a free, unofficial tracker for the campus shuttles in Hyde Park, Chicago, made as a student project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University or by Passio. This policy covers the iPhone app and the web app at blobberus.github.io/straight-bussing.

The short version. No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. The developer collects nothing, receives nothing and has no server. Your live location is never sent anywhere; the only location data that leaves your device is the rounded start and end of a walking leg, used for walking directions.

What stays on your device

  • Your location, if you allow it, is used on your device to find the stops near you and to time walks. The app asks for it only when you tap “Use my location”, only while the app is open, and works without it (search a station or a place instead). You can turn it off at any time: on iPhone in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Straight Bussing; on the web in your browser’s site settings.
  • Your settings (theme, hidden routes, map order, custom routes, favorites, bus alert choices) are saved on your device only: in the app’s storage on iPhone, removed when you delete the app; in this browser’s storage on the web, removed when you clear the site’s data.
  • Place search for station names and places within a 30-minute walk of campus stops runs on your device, including spelling fixes.
  • Bus alerts and the trip Live Activity (iPhone) are created on your device as local notifications. There is no push server. Notifications are optional and the app works without them.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • Shuttle data. The app downloads the public shuttle feeds from passio3.com (vehicle positions, arrival predictions and service alerts). These requests carry no personal data; like any web request, they show your IP address to that server.
  • Place search. Only when the search on your device finds fewer than 5 matches, or when you tap “Search for … instead”, is text sent to photon.komoot.io (OpenStreetMap geocoder by komoot): the text you typed, or its spelling fix when results are shown for the fix, and nothing else. Never your location.
  • Walking directions. Only the start and end of each walking leg (your location, when a trip starts there), rounded to about 10 m, are sent to follow sidewalks: on iPhone to Apple Maps (MapKit), on the web to routing.openstreetmap.de (or valhalla1.openstreetmap.de if that is down). Nothing else about you or your trip is sent. If that fails, a straight-line estimate is used and labeled “estimate”.
  • Maps. On iPhone the map comes from Apple Maps (MapKit), and Apple’s privacy policy applies to it. On the web, map tiles come from tiles.openfreemap.org and the map code from unpkg.com.
  • The web app itself, including its schedule files, is served by GitHub Pages; GitHub’s privacy statement applies to those requests.

These services receive only what is listed above and handle it under their own policies. Straight Bussing contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

Keeping, deleting and changing your mind

  • The developer stores nothing about you, so there is nothing to look up, export or delete on our side.
  • To remove everything the app saved on your device, delete the app (iPhone) or clear this site’s data (web).
  • Location and notifications can be turned off at any time in iOS Settings or your browser; the app keeps working.

Children

The app is for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version needs to send more (for example, bus alerts on a locked phone would need a push server), this page, the About screen and the App Store privacy details will say so before that version ships. The date above shows the last change.

Contact

Questions or concerns about privacy: open an issue at github.com/blobberus/straight-bussing/issues. For help with the app, see the support page.
